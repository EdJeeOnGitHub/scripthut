How these measurements work

Tracked completed and failed jobs, selected by completion time in UTC. Running jobs are not included. CPU efficiency divides total consumed CPU time by total allocated CPU time over the same measured jobs, including failures.

Memory sizing uses comparable peaks from successful jobs. Peak statistics are across jobs, not time averages. Mean RAM over time unavailable. Multi-task peaks may not represent simultaneous whole-job memory. Review OOMs and workload variation before reducing requests.
